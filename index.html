      At the table

        House rules — Five Card 3/2 Draw
        • Deck: 54 cards — the standard 52 plus 2 jokers.
        • Wild: jokers and all deuces (2s) are wild — they can stand for any card.
        • Ante: 10 chips from everyone, every hand.
        • Draws: you draw twice — up to 3 cards after the first betting round, then up to 2 after the second.
        • Betting: three rounds — before the first draw, between the draws, and a final round after the second draw. Raises in 25 / 50 / 100 steps, max 3 raises a round.
        • Five of a kind is the best hand at this table — wild cards make it possible.

        House rules — Blackjack
        • Pays: blackjack pays 3 to 2. Dealer stands on all 17s.
        • Double down on your first two cards — one card, then stand.
        • Seats play in turn: Don, then you, then Layton. The felt follows the turn.
        • Shoe: four decks, reshuffled when it runs low.
        House rules — Texas Hold'em
        • Blinds: 5 / 10 — small blind left of the button, big blind next. Heads-up, the button posts the small blind.
        • Hole cards: 2, private to you. Board: flop (3), turn (1), river (1), shared by everyone.
        • Betting: no-limit in 50 / 100 / 200 raise steps, max 3 raises a round — preflop, flop, turn, river.
        • Showdown: best five of your seven cards (2 hole + 5 board). The board itself can play — then the pot splits.
        • No wilds — a straight 52-card deck.
        House rules — Five Card Draw (classic)
        • Deck: a straight 52 — no jokers, no wilds; deuces are just deuces.
        • Ante: 10 chips from everyone, every hand.
        • One draw only — up to 5 cards after the first betting round, then a final betting round and showdown.
        • Betting: raises in 50 / 100 / 200 steps, max 3 raises a round.

        Play chips only — no real money, no prizes. Just the game.
